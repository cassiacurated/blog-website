Civic Center Farmers Market

★★★★★

Located at Marin Civic Center, this market this open year round Thursdays and Sundays 8am to 1pm.

quality

★★★★★

The quality here is the best I’ve found in Marin so far, a few vendors said the ingredients here are “chef-quality” and I can see why. Both the Thursday and Sunday markets are massive and there are many certified organic, and certified-california grown vendors to choose from, guaranteeing you’ll find what you came for.

variety

★★★★★

The Thursday market has approximately 100-150 vendors while the iconic Sunday market has more like 150-200. Both markets are an experience and great for weekly shopping runs or special occasions. As you can imagine there are many options for produce, animal protein, vegan and artisan food and goods, flowers, ready-made meals, community engagement opportunities, etc. The variety of goods is unmatched.

price

★★★★★

Prices vary throughout the market, I was able to find affordable options for everything on my shopping list, but there are also higher end goods I wasn’t willing to splurge on like nice bottles of wine and California olive oil. Regardless of your budget, this is a great market to incorporate in your routine. As of now, this is my weekly market. I personally prefer the sunday market because it’s fun to be around the large crowds of people but the Thursday market is just as good, just harder to get to if you work.

$-$$$

communication

★★★★★

All the vendors are very welcoming and would love to tell you about their practices. I had long conversations with many of the vendors and feel like I make new friends every time I go. A bunch of the vendors also have take-away pamphlets and/or business cards/social media explaining who they are and where they source their goods. I felt comfortable talking to everyone there. However, there was one guy talking about aliens towards the market entrance… I did not engage :p

atmosphere

★★★★★

I love the atmosphere here, it’s surrounded by trees with views of the iconic Civic Center building designed by architect Frank Lloyd Wright. It’s both a tourist destination and local hot-spot full of energy, live music, dancing, food trucks, and a mosaic of happy shoppers.

final verdict

★★★★★

I give this market 5 stars, both the Thursday and Sunday market have exceptional quality, variety, prices, vendor communication, and atmosphere. Parking can be difficult on Sunday because it’s so popular but if you’re patient you’ll have no problem getting in. I recommend this market to everyone I know!
