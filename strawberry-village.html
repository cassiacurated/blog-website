Strawberry Village Farmers Market

★★★

Located at Strawberry Village Shopping Center, this market is open June-November Tuesdays 10am to 2:30pm.

quality

★★★★★

I feel confident saying the quality of food and artisan goods at this market is high. Almost all the produce is certified organic and comes from local farms that also sell to large grocers in the area. Everything looked fresh, sanitary, and thoughtfully kept. I had no concerns while shopping.

variety

★★★

This is a very intimate collection of approximately 10-15 local vendors. However, there is still a nice variety of goods here including multiple options for: organic produce, baked goods, animal proteins (meat, seafood, cheese), and artisan foods, and flowers. I noticed that Corte Madera and Strawberry Village share the same vendor pool with only a few alternates and overall fewer booths. All organic food is clearly labeled with proper certification. Since this market is seasonally run, there is less variety overall but more ready-made hot food then Corte Madera.

price

★★

Prices were quite high, making this more of a lunch-time treat rather than a go-to grocery store replacement. I would equate the prices here to local high-end groceries such as Good Earth Natural Foods in Mill Valley, or Mollie Stones’ in Sausalito. I personally wouldn’t be able to afford shopping here weekly but would be happy to visit on occasion.

$$-$$$

communication

★★★★

All the vendors are local, and many are family-run. Everyone I met was very friendly, but there were more people than I was expecting which made me feel less comfortable asking thorough questions about vendor practices. Having only been once, I’m not sure if it’s always like that, but the vendors I did speak to welcomed queries. There were also a few friendly visitors who approached me and I was able to talk with them about food and their favorite booths.

atmosphere

★★

The location of this market is in the Strawberry Village shopping center parking lot which has a nostalgic feel to it like traditional farmers markets I grew up going to. However, it is right by the freeway which is loud and a bit obnoxious. There is plenty of space to walk around and there are less retail stores in close proximity compared to Corte Madera. For a Marin County farmers market, I would’ve liked to see more greenery around so this wasn’t my favorite spot. However, the energy was good and I felt a strong sense of community while shopping.

final verdict

★★★

I give this market a total of 3 stars, one for quality, atmosphere, and communication. Prices were even higher than Corte Madera Farmers Market, and the variety of options felt limiting compared to Civic Center Farmers Market. However, the atmosphere was welcoming and friendly like many traditional suburban markets. Parking is easy, but the hours make it hard for anyone with a typical 9-5 schedule to make it there unless they go on a lunch break. It’s convenient but not ideal for weekly groceries unless your schedule and budget allows for it.

$$-$$$
