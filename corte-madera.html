Corte Madera Farmers Market

★★★

Located at Corte Madera Town Center, this market this open year round Wednesdays 12pm to 5pm.

quality

★★★★★

I feel confident saying the quality of food and artisan goods at this market is high. All the produce is certified organic and comes from local farms that also sell to large grocers in the area. Everything looked fresh, sanitary, and thoughtfully kept. I had no concerns while shopping.

variety

★★★

There are approximately 25 local vendors at this market which is relatively small compared to other Bay Area markets. However, there is still a nice variety of goods here including multiple options for: organic produce, baked goods, animal proteins (meat, poultry, seafood, cheese), vegan and artisan foods, and flowers. All organic food is clearly labeled with proper certification. I was most impressed by the number of pastries, bread, pies, cakes, and other sweet treats they had. I wanted to go home with everything! Even with this intimate array of booths, you could definitely shop here for weekly groceries. My only disappointment was the lack of ready-made food options other than packaged sweets. If you come here expecting to get lunch at a food truck you will likely have to settle for one of the restaurants nearby in the shopping center, which in my opinion is less fun.

price

★★

Prices were higher than I expected, but in line with general prices throughout Marin County. I would equate the prices here to local high-end groceries such as Woodland or Nuggets Market. I personally wouldn’t be able to afford shopping here weekly but would be happy to visit on occasion.

$$

communication

★★★★

All the vendors are local, and most are family-run. Everyone I met was very friendly and welcomed me as a newcomer. I was able to ask plenty of questions about the practices of each vendor before buying anything which put me at ease. I don’t think anyone here will have a hard time meeting people or learning about the food.

atmosphere

★★

The location of this market is a little awkward because it’s right in the middle of Corte Madera town center which feels like a suburban strip mall. There is plenty of space to walk around but many of the people around the market were shopping at other businesses, not the farmers market itself. For a Marin County farmers market, I would’ve liked to see more greenery around so this wasn’t my favorite spot. However, the energy was good and everyone seemed to be having a good time.

final verdict

★★★

I give this market a total of 3 stars, one for quality, variety, and communication. I felt the prices were too high and the overall atmosphere was too suburban-industrial for me but this is just one woman’s opinion!
