Field note 001

Hi! I’m Cassia, and I’m exploring Marin County farmers’ markets.


I just moved to Belvedere-Tiburon, one of the many beautiful neighborhoods nestled within Marin County, California. One of the first things I do after a move is find the best local farmers’ markets around me for weekly grocery trips and special treats. There are amazing farmers’ markets all over the Bay Area, with 8 operating just within Marin County. I set out to create this blog to review all the closest farmers’ markets to me as I explore the region. Come along and let’s find good eats in Marin!
